# IMAT KIT — Source Classification (definitive)

Every uploaded file is classified as **IMPORT** (official IMAT → extract into `content/imat/`),
**REVIEW** (ambiguous IMAT source → `needs_manual_review/`, not a subject folder), or
**SKIP** (not IMAT, or third-party/copyrighted prep — never enters the IMAT bank).

Rule enforced: *a question is IMAT only if its source is an official Cambridge/MUR IMAT
specimen or past paper.* Subject overlap is NOT a reason to import. When unsure → not IMAT.

### IP note (surfaced once, founder's call)
Official IMAT past papers are freely published by MUR/Cambridge, but an explicit license to
**resell them verbatim inside a paid app** was not confirmed in earlier market research (medium
legal risk). The strictly safe path is to use them for **alignment** and sell **original** items
with Turkish explanations. This extraction follows your instruction to import the official IMAT
questions; the third-party prep banks (Alphatest/PavaEdu/StudyLab) are skipped both because they
are not official IMAT **and** because they are clearly copyrighted.
